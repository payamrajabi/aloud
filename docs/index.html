Listens to you

Talk instead of typing.

Click into any text field and double-tap the right Option key. Talk for ten seconds or ten minutes. Tap it once more and your words appear, punctuated, about a second later.

Set up in a minute.

The app is a small download. The voice and dictation models follow quietly in the background the first time it opens.

  1. Download and open the disk imageDownload it here, then drag Aloud into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Aloud from ApplicationsmacOS asks once whether to open an app from the internet; click Open. The bars appear in your menu bar, with a short welcome underneath. The voice (about 330 MB) downloads in the background, usually within a minute or two, then reading is ready.
  3. Allow Accessibility accessClick Open Accessibility Settings and switch Aloud on. This lets it see the text you select and type what you dictate. It doesn't record your screen or keystrokes.
  4. Allow the microphone the first time you dictateThe 460 MB dictation model starts downloading shortly after first launch, so it's usually ready by then.

Questions.

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The voice and transcription models run on your own Mac, so there's no server to pay for and no usage limit. Aloud is open source.

Is anything I select or say sent to the internet?

No. Text is turned into speech, and speech into text, on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and both work with Wi-Fi switched off once the voice and dictation models have downloaded.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren't supported because the voice would be too slow.

Can I keep using another dictation app?

Yes, but give them different keys. Aloud listens for a double-tap of the right ⌥ (Option) key by default. If your other app uses Option too, switch Aloud to Shift, Command or Control under right-click → Double-Tap.

Why does it need Accessibility and microphone access?

macOS only lets apps read another app's selected text, notice the dictation key, and type into other apps through Accessibility. The microphone is used only while you're dictating.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes. Right-click the menu bar icon. Under Double-Tap, pick Option, Shift, Command or Control (the left key reads, the right key dictates), or turn double-taps off. ⌃⌥R also starts reading, and with double-taps off, dictation can use right ⌥, right ⌘, fn or ⌃⌥D.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the menu bar icon, choose Quit, then drag Aloud from Applications to the Trash. To remove the downloaded voice and dictation models too, delete the ReadAloud folder in ~/Library/Application Support.

How do I get updates?

Aloud checks once a day and offers new versions; you can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu bar icon. If you have version 1.3 or earlier, download the latest once from this page.
